PistonHeads landing page: redesign options

Two alternatives are below, shown next to a copy of the live page. All three are working prototypes with the same advert data (advert 21270720, 2021 BMW M135i).

What we found

The two pages are not the same

CarGurus says getting a quote won’t impact your credit score. PistonHeads swaps that for “No deposit options available”, which speaks to budget buyers and drops the main reassurance.

The car is an afterthought

The advert photo is a 90 × 70px thumbnail that loads late, so first paint shows a gap. Visitors come from a specific car, and the page opens with a pitch about a broker.

Button height is only part of it

The live button is already inside a 667px-tall screen. Both redesigns lift it by about 160px, to 443px, and add a sticky Continue bar that is always on screen.

Wording rules for the redesigns

  • No rate, savings, monthly-payment or credit-score claims. Every line is either already on the live page or in the approved consumer proof points, so none should need new sign-off. Compliance should still confirm before launch.
  • All legal text is kept word for word and moved below the button.
